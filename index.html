How I Create

The journey from vision to masterpiece

01

Pour & Flow

Resin Art

Each resin piece begins with carefully selected pigments and metallic powders. I heat, mix, and pour layers of crystal-clear epoxy, manipulating depth and movement with a torch to create organic, ocean-inspired patterns. The curing process takes 48 hours of patience.

02

Curate & Layer

Gift Hampers

Every hamper tells a story. I begin by sourcing premium elements — artisan treats, hand-tied ribbons, dried botanicals. The arrangement follows a layered composition principle: anchoring larger items at the base, building texture in the middle, and crowning with decorative accents.

03

Trace & Color

Rangoli

Rangoli is meditation in motion. I start from the center outward, sketching geometric guides with chalk before filling intricate patterns with vibrant powders. Each design can take 3–6 hours, demanding steady hands and deep focus to achieve perfect symmetry.

04

Move & Express

Choreography

Choreography starts with the music — I listen repeatedly until I feel the rhythm in my body. Then comes the mapping of formations, transitions, and emotional peaks. Rehearsals refine raw expression into polished performance, blending classical technique with contemporary energy.
